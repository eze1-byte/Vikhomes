Common questions

Which thickness should I choose?

3/4" suits heavy-duty shelves and frames, 1/2" suits general furniture, and 1/4" suits backing and lining. Send us your project on WhatsApp and we'll advise.

Can I use ordinary plywood outdoors?

It is meant for dry indoor use. For damp areas, ask us about marine plywood.
